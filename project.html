Project Context

Existing community. Defined rebuild scope.

Seventeen units at 1900–1932 Palisades Drive were total losses in the January 2025 fire. Eight additional units in the same community remain intact and are outside the primary rebuild scope.

Original architectural and building plans, site layout and unit information are available. The transaction and reconstruction are being organized as two separate stages.

Two-Stage Structure

Control first. Construction second.

Stage 1

Acquisition / Control

Up to ~$8.0M

Working capital for securing control of the 17 fire-loss units and resolving transaction-side items, including seller agreements, mortgage payoffs, liens, title matters, HOA / legal coordination, closing costs and related diligence.

Stage 1 is currently expected to begin only after the current diligence and structuring period is completed. The ~$8M figure is a working capital requirement, not a stated purchase price.

Stage 2

Reconstruction / Construction Financing

Debt + Equity Structure TBD
