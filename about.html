About Pegasus

Pegasus Lenders Group LLC.

Pegasus Lenders Group LLC is the capital-facing organization for select real estate opportunities. It develops investor and lender relationships, evaluates capital structures, coordinates debt and equity discussions, and communicates directly with capital partners.

California Residential Development Partners provides the complementary development and construction execution platform. Together, the platform combines capital structuring with hands-on project delivery.

Pegasus is not a marketplace, crowdfunding platform, investor portal, or payment system. Every opportunity is reviewed through direct, human-led conversations.
