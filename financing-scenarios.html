Before You Submit

Clarity matters more than volume.

Strong submissions include

  • Property and location
  • Purpose and requested amount
  • Current basis and existing debt
  • Sponsor experience
  • Closing timeline and exit

Do not submit here

  • Tax returns or bank statements
  • Government identification
  • Account or Social Security numbers
  • Confidential legal documents
  • Unredacted borrower records
