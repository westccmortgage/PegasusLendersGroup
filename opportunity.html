Investment Thesis

The value-creation thesis begins with control.

The objective of Stage 1 is to convert a fragmented post-loss ownership situation into a controlled, financeable reconstruction project.

This is not a blind pool or an undefined ground-up concept. The asset, unit count, original documentation, and reconstruction scope are identifiable today.

  1. 01Fragmented post-loss ownership
  2. 02Consolidate control
  3. 03Create a financeable reconstruction opportunity
  4. 04Execute reconstruction
  5. 05Disposition / exit

Sponsor / Capital Partner Structure

Seeking a lead capital partner for Stage 1.

Pegasus is prepared to evaluate project-level equity, preferred equity, or another mutually acceptable structure. Exact economics would be negotiated around the final verified Stage 1 requirement, timing, investor protections, and the transition into construction financing.

Sponsor alignmentEconomically aligned

Sponsor cash is intended to represent a minority portion of acquisition/control capital rather than the primary funding source. No fixed sponsor percentage is represented.
