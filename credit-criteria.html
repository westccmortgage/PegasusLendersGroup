Private Credit Criteria

What comes before a capital conversation.

Every scenario is reviewed individually. These are screening factors, not published lending guidelines or a commitment to fund.

01

Bridge

Time-sensitive acquisition, transition, or payoff scenarios.

02

Construction

Ground-up, completion, and substantial rehabilitation capital.

03

Acquisition

Business-purpose real estate purchases with a defined execution plan.

04

Multifamily

Apartment acquisition, repositioning, renovation, and recapitalization.

05

Commercial

Selected owner-user, investment, mixed-use, and transitional assets.

06

Structured Capital

Preferred or project-specific structures reviewed individually.

01

Collateral

Asset type, location, condition, title, and marketability.

02

Basis

Purchase price, current investment, budget, and verified uses.

03

Leverage

Requested proceeds relative to as-is value, cost, and completed value.

04

Execution

Sponsor experience, team, approvals, construction plan, and controls.

05

Timing

Closing need, project milestones, term, extension, and capital duration.

06

Exit

Sale, refinance, stabilization, repayment source, and downside plan.
