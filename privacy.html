Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026.

Information we collect

We collect the business contact and scenario information that you choose to submit, together with basic source and attribution data such as landing page, referrer, and campaign parameters.

How we use it

We use submitted information to review the requested business contact, evaluate potential relationship fit, prevent abuse, maintain records, and improve the website. We do not request sensitive personal financial documents through public forms.

Sharing and retention

Information may be shared with service providers that operate the website and with affiliated or professional parties when necessary to evaluate the specific request and permitted by law. Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for these purposes, legal obligations, and dispute prevention.

Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of information you submitted by contacting contact@pegasuslendersgroup.com, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

Security

No public website can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit account numbers, tax identifiers, government identification, bank statements, tax returns, or other sensitive records through these forms.
