# Pegasus Lenders Group — durable instructions

## Canonical project

- Website: https://pegasuslendersgroup.com
- Repository: https://github.com/westccmortgage/PegasusLendersGroup
- Keep all production work in this repository.

## Business model

Pegasus Lenders Group is a human-led private real estate credit and capital-relationship desk. Its public website connects:

- capital providers who publish real lending criteria; and
- sponsors or borrowers who submit defined business-purpose real estate financing scenarios.

It is not presented as a bank, depository institution, registered broker-dealer, investment adviser, crowdfunding portal, securities exchange, escrow, or automated underwriting platform. It does not accept money, loan commitments, investments, sensitive financial documents, or payments through the website. Do not promise financing, matching, returns, closings, or deal volume.

Do not present Vista Del Mar, Pacific Palisades, or any other project as a current or confirmed fundraising opportunity without a new explicit owner instruction.

## Ecosystem boundaries

- Pegasus Capital Network: voluntary profiles, criteria discovery, and member-directed introductions.
- Pegasus Lenders Group: specialized private real estate credit relationships and scenario review.
- West Coast Capital Investment: project-specific opportunity review and structuring across real estate, private credit, and select ventures.
- California RDP: development and construction execution.

Keep the roles separate and describe participation only when the relevant party has opted in.

## Public experience

Primary paths:

1. Publish Your Lending Criteria.
2. Submit a Financing Scenario.

The process is human-led: intake, review, clarification, criteria comparison, direct introduction when appropriate. Do not build payments, subscriptions, investor accounts, accreditation automation, KYC/AML workflows, securities checkout, dashboards, or automated qualification.

## Content and design

Use short, precise copy. The visitor should understand the purpose quickly.

Visual language: warm white, light stone, deep navy, graphite, restrained steel blue. Institutional, direct, financially serious, modern, and clean. No gold, crypto aesthetic, neon, or luxury-club styling.

Project references may be used only as limited proof of execution unless the owner explicitly approves a live opportunity. Never publish private ownership, financing, cost, or return details without approval.

## Forms and compliance

Use Netlify Forms. Collect only business-contact and high-level criteria/scenario information. Do not request SSNs, bank statements, tax returns, government IDs, passwords, wiring instructions, or uploads of sensitive documents.

Required legal pages: Privacy, Terms, and Disclosures. Every form must include consent language and must not create a commitment.
