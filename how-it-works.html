How It Works

Direct relationships. Defined steps.

Pegasus is not a public securities marketplace, automated approval system, or payment platform.

01

Introduce

A capital provider publishes criteria or a sponsor submits a defined scenario.

02

Screen

Pegasus reviews fit, missing facts, timing, and the appropriate relationship path.

03

Match

Only relevant criteria and scenarios advance to a direct conversation.

04

Discuss

The parties review structure, diligence needs, and whether to proceed.

05

Document

Any loan, investment, or project relationship is documented by the appropriate parties.

Boundaries

What the website does not do.

  • No automatic loan approvals
  • No securities checkout
  • No custody of funds
  • No guaranteed matching
  • No investment advice
  • No public deal negotiation
  • No sensitive-document upload
  • No commitment through a form
